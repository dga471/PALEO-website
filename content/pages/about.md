## Mission

The Passive Asynchronous Lattice Exposure Observatory (PALEO) brings together
particle physicists, geoscientists and materials scientists to develop
paleo-detectors: natural minerals whose crystal lattices have recorded the
passage of charged particles over geological timescales. The collaboration
aims to establish the technique as a tool for dark matter searches and for
neutrino physics, from sample selection and dating through track readout and
analysis.

## Organisation

PALEO has around 45 members from 11 institutions in the United States, the
United Kingdom, Switzerland and Slovenia. The spokespersons are Patrick Huber
(Virginia Tech) and Chris Kelso (University of North Florida).
