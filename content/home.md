PALEO (Passive Asynchronous Lattice Exposure Observatory) is a collaboration of physicists, geologists, and material scientists searching for dark matter and neutrinos using ancient minerals. Over geological time, rare particle interactions leave damage tracks in the crystal lattice of a mineral. By reading out those tracks with modern microscopy, a rock that has been buried for hundreds of millions of years becomes a particle detector with an exposure no laboratory experiment can match.
